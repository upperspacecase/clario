# CMO Angle — Clario

## Lens applied
- **Godin (Smallest Viable Audience):** Not "SMEs." Not even "restaurant owners." Start with Spanish-speaking restaurant owners in Spain + LatAm who run 1–3 locations and have never used an AI tool beyond maybe Google Translate. Everyone else is bonus.
- **Dry test (could a competitor sign this?):** Nothing generic like "AI for your business." The word "restaurant" has to appear above the fold, in the language of the caller.

## Headline (primary, English)
**Five minutes on the phone. Three AI tools that actually fit your restaurant.**

- Visualizable (you can picture the call).
- Verifiable (there's a phone call; there are three tools; the caller can count).
- No competitor is shipping a voice-first AI advisor for non-English-speaking restaurant owners. Hard to sign.

## Subhead
Clario calls you back, listens to how your restaurant actually runs — reservations, reviews, waste, staff — and sends a plain-language report of AI tools you can try this week. In Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Vietnamese, or the language your kitchen already speaks.

## Alt headlines (in the language of the audience, rotated in hero badge)
- ES: *Cinco minutos al teléfono. Tres herramientas de IA para su restaurante.*
- PT: *Cinco minutos ao telefone. Três ferramentas de IA para o seu restaurante.*
- IT: *Cinque minuti al telefono. Tre strumenti di IA per il tuo ristorante.*
- VI: *Năm phút trên điện thoại. Ba công cụ AI cho nhà hàng của bạn.*

## Primary CTA
**Leave your number — we'll call you back in your language.**

- Button label: `Get my call back`
- Fields: phone, language dropdown (ES, PT, IT, VI, FR, DE, other), country, restaurant name (optional).
- Commitment device: "We call within 24 hours, or we don't ask for your number again."

## Value prop above the fold (3 bullets — eye-motion supports them)
- **No English needed.** Speak however you speak to your suppliers.
- **No jargon.** You describe your week, we translate it into 3 tools.
- **No consultant fees.** Free during early access.

## Honesty caveats (mandatory, per skill rules)
- Under the logo strip: **"Not affiliated. Tools Clario may recommend based on your call."**
- Hero badge: **"Pre-launch — early-access waitlist."**

## Objection pre-empting (woven in, not a section)
- "Will my data be used to train an AI?" → in the footer link "How we handle your call."
- "Do I have to install anything?" → The subhead answers this: it's a phone call.

## What the Remotion demo must prove
A Spanish-speaking restaurant owner's voice (waveform animating) describes: *"Tengo un restaurante familiar en Sevilla. Pierdo muchas reservas los domingos y no sé cómo responder a las reseñas de Google…"* — and three AI tool cards appear one after the other: **OpenTable / Square reservations**, **Google Reviews auto-reply**, **Canva social posts**. Each card has one line in Spanish saying what it fixes. Loop ends on "Report sent. 14:02."

This is the proof. If the demo doesn't show a non-English caller getting specific tools back, the page fails its own promise.
